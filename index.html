About Fund Connect

Fund Connect is a proposed worldwide crowdfunding platform to help people explain meaningful causes, share progress and connect with supporters.

Our principles

  • Clear and truthful campaign information.
  • Respect for donor and campaign privacy.
  • Transparent budgets and updates.
  • Campaign review and safety checks.

Before a real launch

The platform will need adult-led legal and business review, secure hosting, payment partners, identity and campaign checks, privacy protections, fraud controls and clear refund procedures. Rules and payment availability vary by country.
